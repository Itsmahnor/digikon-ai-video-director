# Rubric

**Technical (probes in code):** duration vs plan, resolution, frame rate, audio stream present, black frames, frozen frames, silence, loudness vs target, caption overflow.

**Visual (frames):**
- Product fidelity: shape, colour, label and logo match the supplied assets.
- Brand fidelity: palette, logo placement and tone match the brand rules.
- Pacing: no scene lingers without purpose; no hold shorter than it can be read.
- Continuity: screen direction and lighting consistent across adjacent shots.
- Captions: inside the safe area, readable, on screen long enough.
- Transitions: match the plan; no glitches at the cut.
- Audio: music ducks under speech; no clipping.

Each item in the output is a finding with evidence, or is recorded as passed with evidence.
