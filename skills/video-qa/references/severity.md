# Severity and owner

- **critical:** unplayable output, wrong product, unsupported claim shown, missing audio when expected. Blocks export.
- **major:** visible defect, cut or caption problem, loudness out of range. Needs revision.
- **minor:** small polish issue. Revise if cheap.
- **info:** observation only.

Owner skill: framing and shot choice -> cinematography; cuts, timing and transitions -> editing-director; text and animation -> motion-graphics; audio -> sound-design; claims or message -> commercial-strategy.
