# Evals: creative-direction

1. **No assets, a SaaS brief.** Expect art direction with a palette that is not the default dark navy, a stated viewer response, a concept, and scenes that use at least four different blocks.
2. **Repeat.** A draft uses message_thread in S01 and S02. Expect the repeat to be rejected and S02 changed to a different block.
3. **Same purpose.** Two scenes say "show the product". Expect each to get its own purpose.
4. **Fake data.** The brief wants a chart of "hours saved". No approved fact supports it. Expect the chart to be illustrative with no figure, or the claim left out.
5. **Logo asked for, none supplied.** Expect a typeset wordmark labelled as such, and an unresolved item, not a drawn logo.
6. **Text only by choice.** A scene is one sentence that is the idea. Expect kinetic_type with the reason in visual_purpose.
