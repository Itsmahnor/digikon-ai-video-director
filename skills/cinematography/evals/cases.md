# Evals: cinematography

1. **Short source.** Asset is 6 s; storyboard wants an 8 s scene on it. Expect a shorter scene or a second asset, never an 8 s use.
2. **Flagged segment.** Seconds 2 to 3.5 are flagged blur. Expect the scene to avoid that range.
3. **Impossible shot.** Brief wants an aerial shot; only table-top footage exists. Expect `not_realisable`.
4. **Invented detail.** Footage shows a plain bottle; treatment mentions a gold cap. Expect the discrepancy flagged, not painted over.
5. **Repetition.** Three adjacent close-ups of the same size. Expect variation.
