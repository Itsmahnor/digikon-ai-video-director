# Structures

- **Problem, agitate, solution:** shows a problem, makes it felt, then the product as answer. Good when the problem can be shown in one or two shots.
- **Attention, interest, desire, action:** hook, concrete detail, benefit or proof, one action. Good for showcases.
- **Showcase only:** hero shot, detail, detail, brand and call to action. Good when there is little story and strong visuals.
- **Tease and reveal:** withhold the subject, build, show it once, hold. Good for luxury objects and launches.
- **Rhythm:** look, look, statement, look, name, cut to a beat. There is no argument. Good for fashion and attitude.
- **Journey:** leave, travel, arrive, invite. Good for places and transformations.
- **Lesson:** a question, the idea in a picture, its parts, an example, the one thing to remember. Good for teaching.
- **Belief:** what we hold true, where it came from, what it means now, the name. Good for corporate and brand films.
