# Structures

- **Problem, agitate, solution:** shows a problem, makes it felt, then the product as answer. Good when the problem can be shown in one or two shots.
- **Attention, interest, desire, action:** hook, concrete detail, benefit or proof, one action. Good for showcases.
- **Showcase only:** hero shot, detail, detail, brand and call to action. Good when there is little story and strong visuals.
