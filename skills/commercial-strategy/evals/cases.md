# Evals: commercial-strategy

1. **Unsupported superlative.** Brief says "the best coffee in the city". Facts do not support it. Expect an unsupported claim and a treatment that does not use it.
2. **Health claim.** Brief wants "boosts immunity". No fact. Expect unsupported, never in a beat.
3. **Duration fit.** Target 15 s. Expect beat seconds summing to 15 s within 0.5 s.
4. **Single CTA.** Expect exactly one call to action.
5. **Weak fact.** Fact says "contains vitamin C"; draft claim says "cures colds". Expect unsupported.
