## Retrieval limitations discovered

- No relevance threshold: 2 of 19 negative queries still returned documents (ng-01, ng-03) because any shared term gives a positive BM25 score. Production needs a score floor or a reranker to say "nothing suitable".
- Vocabulary mismatch: 5 retrieval queries share no stemmed term with their relevant document (sr-18, sr-19, pp-12, pp-15, pp-24). Only dense retrieval can recover these.
- Outranking: 14 retrieval queries had a relevant document that shares terms with the query but ranked below a wrong one (seed-18, dp-04, pp-03, pp-07, pp-09, pp-10, pp-21, pp-22, sl-02, sl-03, sl-14, sl-17, sl-23, sl-24).
- Recipe titles are indexed twice and 'Used for' phrases are keyword lists, so queries phrased like those lists succeed while paraphrases depend on shared stems.
- Renderer variants (-hyperframes, -remotion) duplicate their base recipe text, so a plain query cannot distinguish them; the benchmark treats them as equivalent unless the query names the renderer.
- The tokenizer is English-only suffix stripping with no synonyms, so 'dissolve' will not match 'crossfade' unless the document lists it.
- 3 of 6 collections are empty (platform_specs, brand_product_facts, approved_examples), so retrieval quality there cannot be measured yet.
