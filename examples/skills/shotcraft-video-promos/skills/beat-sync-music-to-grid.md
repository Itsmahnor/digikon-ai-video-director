**When to use:** a promo cut to a music track.

**Method:** measure the beat grid by fitting evenly spaced beats to the detected beat times (the reported tempo can be off by a couple of percent). Classify hits and map the energy structure (where energy fills, where it breaks down) and the two or three strongest hits for the opening, peak and ending. Write the timeline in beat numbers, not frames. Verify after render by extracting the audio and comparing cut times to real transients.

**Rule:** beat hits go on elements (card, title, number); full-frame impacts are rare.
