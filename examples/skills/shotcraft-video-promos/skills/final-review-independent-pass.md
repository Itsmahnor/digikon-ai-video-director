**When to use:** before declaring any promo finished.

**Checks (reviewer who did not build it):** product goal met; every planned feature present; visual direction held; shot choices consistent with the storyboard; data and assets safe (no personal data, licences recorded); audio and rhythm; technical quality of frames (sharp text, no black or frozen frames).

**Output:** a report listing pass and fail per check with the frame or time of each problem. Anything failing is fixed and re-checked.

**Rule:** the reviewer must not be the person who built the film, and the review is written, with a time or frame for each problem.

**Claims:** the reviewer checks each statement in the film against the supplied facts and records any unsupported claim as a failure, not a note.
