**When to use:** hero shots of a physical product and the closing beat.

**Close-up kit:** a tilted side angle, a sense of height, a slow orbit, and a dark contrasting material background so the product edge reads. **Finale:** end with the brand wordmark as the focal point while the film's elements converge around it, peaking in energy, and hold the mark.

**Claims warning:** show only the product as supplied; do not alter colours, add features or invent packaging.

**Rule:** keep the close-up simple (one product, one move, one background), and let the finale be the only moment where many elements appear together.
