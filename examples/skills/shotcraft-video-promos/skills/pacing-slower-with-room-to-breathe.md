**When to use:** timing any promo, especially first drafts.

**Rules:** after information-carrying elements settle (a title card, a number, the full grid), hold still for at least one second before cutting; give that pause to brand memory points first (the wordmark lands and holds a full second), not to ordinary cards. Speed comes from acceleration, not constant speed: use non-linear easing and uneven stagger, since constant-speed motion reads as a cheap slideshow; for many similar elements entering, accelerate them with a physical metaphor (like dealing cards) and hold still about half a second when full. Default slower than instinct: the opening action arc is at least about three seconds, and interactive demos run at a real person's speed so a viewer could follow along. Reviews in practice almost always ask for slower, not faster.

**Example:** the wordmark lands at the start and the end and holds one full second each time.

**Check:** watch the draft as a first-time viewer; where something was missed, slow it down, and where nothing happens, cut it.
