**When to use:** timing any promo, especially first drafts.

**Rules:** after key information lands, let it breathe; hold a brand wordmark for a full second. Speed comes from acceleration, not constant fast motion; end with a short half-second pause. Default slower than instinct: main action arcs of at least about three seconds, and UI demonstrations at the speed of a real person using it. Limit full-frame beat hits to a handful in the whole film and prefer moving elements over the whole picture.

**Example:** a price card eases in over 0.8 s, holds 1.2 s, then the next beat starts.
