**When to use:** adding sound effects or music to a film.

**Rules:** keep the sound timeline as one central table of pinned effects, not audio inside scene components. Choose by real sound source: whoosh for camera moves, impact for landing, riser for build-up, sparkle for glints, transition sounds for scene changes, plus real-sounding foley for distinctive actions. Judge candidates in the context of the film; download counts, ratings and waveforms are screening evidence, not proof that you listened. Download into the project, keep the manifest and the credits file with the files, and never fetch audio at render time. Music has its own licence; do not apply a sound-effect licence to it. Gain values are multipliers, not loudness targets; check clipping and whether voice and effects are masked.

**Ask first:** which music the user wants; if none, pick by product mood and say so.

**Check:** every sound file has a manifest entry with its licence and any credit text before the film is delivered.
