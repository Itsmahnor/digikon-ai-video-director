**When to use:** adding sound effects to a promo or UI film.

**Method:** derive search terms from the picture, check candidates for repetition, and judge each UI sound by its timbre. Pin each sound to a frame, duration and volume relative to its shot start. Avoid a machine-gun effect on repeated actions by varying or thinning. Close a shot with the riser-impact-sparkle phrase. Re-check sound after any visual rework, and account for the pipeline's audio offset.

**Licence:** record each sound's licence and credit requirement; do not use audio whose licence is unknown.

**Check:** mute each effect in turn; if the shot loses nothing, the effect is not needed.
