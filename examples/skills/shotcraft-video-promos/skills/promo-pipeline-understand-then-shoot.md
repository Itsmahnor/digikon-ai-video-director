**When to use:** a full product promo for a web or desktop product.

**Stages:** (0) product understanding and execution limits; (1) visual direction and one styleframe, placed on an energy axis (calm premium to high-energy) and a tone axis (serious to playful); (2) map product features to shots; (3) storyboard that becomes the production sign-off; (4) capture final assets; (5) implement shot by shot with stills checked at fixed frames; (6) sound design; (7) independent final review.

**Rules:** acceptance runs through the whole pipeline, not only at the end. Rendering must be deterministic: no wall-clock time or unseeded randomness. Do not skip the read-only product check when the user gives a URL or recording.

**Caution:** the build looks only at real captures supplied or approved by the user.
