**When to use:** a strong-beat track on a promo.

**Rule:** cutting on the beat does not mean hitting the picture on every beat. Impacts on the whole frame or camera (scale pump, shake, flash frames, negative frames) count as big slams: at most three in the whole film, pinned to the strongest hits of the track, with at least about 16 beats between two of them. Every other beat accent acts only on a hero element (a card, title or number). Beat-pump runs are limited to a few beats at a time and at most once per film.

**Why:** hitting the whole picture on every kick makes the frame shake at the beat's frequency and hurts viewing.

**Check:** play eight consecutive beats of the high-energy section frame by frame; if the whole picture pulses on each beat, remove most of them.
