**When to use:** opening shots and any product close-up.

**Rules:** open on a single protagonist with a complete action arc rather than a crowd of elements dancing. Keep the camera stable; no handheld shake by default in product promos. Highlights and sweeps are used sparingly: at most one per shot, on the hero only, and clipped to its rounded edges. Fly-ins land in real layout slots so elements never float above the page.

**Example:** the dashboard card flies in, settles into its slot, one soft highlight crosses it once.
