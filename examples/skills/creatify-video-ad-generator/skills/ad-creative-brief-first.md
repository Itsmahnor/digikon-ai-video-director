**When to use:** at the start of any paid or organic ad, before script or storyboard.

**Structure:** one page, five blocks. Product and price; target audience (main pain, current alternative, objections); positioning (one-sentence core benefit, at most three supporting benefits, proof points, the unique mechanism); specification (platform, aspect ratio, length, tone, the single CTA); creative direction (style, must-include, must-avoid, reference ads).

**Why it matters:** it forces strategy before production. A missing block is a question for the user, never something to fill with a guess.

**Claims warning:** proof points (reviews, statistics, awards) must come from facts the user supplied. If none are given, write "no proof supplied" and design the ad without a numeric claim.

**Example:** a SaaS invoicing ad whose brief shows pain = chasing late payments, mechanism = automatic reminders, proof = none supplied. The ad leads with the pain and the mechanism, and shows no percentage.
