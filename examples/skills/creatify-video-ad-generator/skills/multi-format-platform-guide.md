**When to use:** when a campaign must run on more than one placement.

**Structure:** build the 9:16 hero first, then adapt. 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts; 1:1 or 4:5 for feed placements; 16:9 for YouTube and landscape LinkedIn; 1:1 is the safest single asset for everywhere.

**Typical lengths:** TikTok and Reels 15 to 30 s; feed 15 s; Shorts 30 to 45 s; YouTube pre-roll 6 to 15 s non-skippable, 15 or 30 s otherwise; LinkedIn about 15 s. Many placements autoplay muted, so always make a captions-baked version.

**Pacing:** shorter cuts and a faster hook on vertical feeds; allow a slower build on 16:9.

**Claims warning:** platform limits change. Treat these figures as defaults and confirm the current spec before delivery.

**Example:** one 9:16 master at 20 s, cropped to 4:5 with text re-checked for safe zones, plus a 15 s 16:9 cutdown for pre-roll.
