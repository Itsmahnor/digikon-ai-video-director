**When to use:** after the hook is chosen, to pick the body of a 15 to 30 second ad.

**Structures with timing:**
- *Problem-Agitate-Solve:* problem 2-3 s, agitate 3-5 s (the consequence of not solving it), solve 5-8 s. For pain-driven products and productivity tools.
- *Feature cascade:* hero feature 3-4 s, two supporting features 2-3 s each, proof 2-3 s. For SaaS and feature-rich products.
- *Social proof stack:* real customer words 3-4 s, visual proof 3-4 s, volume proof 2-3 s, urgency 2-3 s. For competitive markets.
- *Before/after:* before 3-5 s, transformation 2-3 s, after 3-5 s, how to get it 2-3 s. For visible outcomes.
- *Day in the life:* routine 3-4 s, the aha moment 3-4 s, the feeling 3-4 s, invitation 2-3 s. For lifestyle and UGC-style ads.

**Rule:** choose one structure per ad and match it to the goal and the proof you actually have.

**Claims:** only claims backed by facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials. Social proof, volume proof and urgency beats are only allowed when real material was supplied; otherwise switch structure.

**Example:** a SaaS with no customer quotes uses the feature cascade and ends on a live demo as proof.
