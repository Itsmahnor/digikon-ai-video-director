**When to use:** sceptical or B2B audiences where credibility matters more than surprise.

**Patterns:** "[Number] people can't be wrong"; "Experts say [claim], here is why"; "After testing [number] options, this one won". Best for health and beauty, B2B and premium products.

**Rule:** the number, the credential and the test must be real and sourced. If the user supplied a count, a rating or a named credential, use it exactly as given. If not, use a different hook (a visible result or a question).

**Claims warning:** this is the riskiest hook pattern for invented statistics and for regulated claims. Flag expert or "clinically proven" wording for review and never imply an endorsement by a named expert without permission.

**Example:** "Rated 4.8 by 1,200 customers" only when the rating and count came from the user.
