**When to use:** when the user will run paid variants.

**Structure:** priority order of what to test: hook, CTA, format, length, presenter, music. Change one variable per test. Plan three to five hooks over the same body.

**Rules of thumb:** give each variant enough spend and impressions before judging, run at least several days to cover weekday variance, and read hook rate (three-second views over impressions) first.

**Claims warning:** do not promise results. Report what was tested, not what "works".

**Example:** four hooks (question, claim, interrupt, result) over one fixed 20-second body and the same CTA.
