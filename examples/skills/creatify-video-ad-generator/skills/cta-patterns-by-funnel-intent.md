**When to use:** when writing or reviewing the closing beat of any ad.

**Structure:** one CTA only, matched to intent.
- Direct ("Shop now"): clear purchase intent, e-commerce.
- Curiosity ("See how it works"): consideration stage.
- Urgency ("Ends Sunday"): only with a real deadline or stock limit the user confirmed.
- Risk reversal ("Try it free"): SaaS and high-price items, only if a free trial or guarantee actually exists.
- Social ("Join the teams already using it"): only with a supplied customer count.
- Benefit ("Start invoicing in minutes"): transformation products.

**Pacing:** hold the CTA on screen long enough to read twice, about 2 to 3 seconds, with the brand mark.

**Claims warning:** never invent urgency, scarcity, trial length, guarantees or customer counts.

**Example:** SaaS, consideration stage, curiosity CTA "See a 2-minute demo" over the logo and URL.
