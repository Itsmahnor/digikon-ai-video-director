**When to use:** before scripting any promotional video, to fix what is being sold and what the viewer does next.

**Structure:** record the single current offer (name, price, where, one-sentence outcome, deliverables flagship first, bonuses, guarantee) and a list of what is not sold. State the funnel in one line per step and the CTA chain. Every video reinforces at least one belief shift and ends on that one next step.

**Claims warning:** keep a proof bank (results, member wins) and verify each item before reuse. If the offer, price or guarantee is missing, ask the user; never invent them.

**Example:** offer "Starter plan, 14-day trial", chain "demo video, then trial page", belief shift "invoicing does not need a spreadsheet".

**Check:** after one viewing, could someone state the offer and the next step? If not, remove content until they can.
