**When to use:** a 30 to 90 second product, how-it-works or onboarding explainer.

**Structure:** an explainer is an argument, not a feature tour. State one core idea in one sentence first; cut what does not serve it. Write the voice-over first, then storyboard. Arc: problem (name the pain in their words), stakes (what it costs), solution (one line), how it works (one to three concrete steps), payoff and a single CTA. Pick one analogy from the viewer's world and keep it throughout.

**Pacing:** about 2.3 spoken words per second, so a 60 s video is roughly 138 words.

**Claims warning:** stakes (time or money lost) must be backed by supplied facts, or phrased as a feeling, not a statistic.

**Example:** core idea "late payments are a process problem"; analogy a queue; five beats mapped to 5, 8, 10, 25 and 12 seconds.
