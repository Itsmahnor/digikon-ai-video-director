**When to use:** when editing or timing an explainer.

**Structure:** pace tracks tension. Problem and stakes are brisk, with faster cuts and short holds. The solution gets one clear hold, a beat of relief. How-it-works is the slowest section: one step at a time, held long enough to read. The payoff breathes, and the end card holds with one CTA.

**Pacing:** narration drives; each visual illustrates the line being spoken and never leads it.

**Example:** a 60 s video with 2-second cuts in the first 15 s, 5-second holds on each of three steps, and a 4-second end card.
