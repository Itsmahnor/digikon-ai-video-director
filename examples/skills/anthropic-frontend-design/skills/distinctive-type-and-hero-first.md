**General visual-design skill (not video-specific); useful for landing-page hero loops and title cards.**

**Principles:** identify the actual subject, audience and the one thing they must do before designing. Open with the most characteristic thing in the subject's world (a headline, an image, an animation, a live demo). Typography carries personality: one family or two clearly distinct ones, chosen deliberately with a clear scale, and line lengths under about 80 characters. Avoid the usual generated-design tells, such as accenting a single word of every headline.

**Rule:** brand fonts and colours from the user override these defaults.
