**When to use:** a 15 to 30 second launch, feature demo or before/after clip for social and landing pages.

**Structure:** gather the brief, brand tokens and a real screen recording first. Then write a shot table (beat, time, visual, text overlay) before any build. Lead with the viewer's problem, not the product name. The product appears within three seconds. Make before/after the money shot and design the whole clip around it.

**Pacing:** 15 to 30 s; text readable at 720p phone size; most social views are muted, so burn captions in and skip voice-over unless requested.

**Claims warning:** use the real product UI copy only. Invented labels, feature names or agent names are the main reason a launch feels fake. Never show a feature that does not exist.

**Example:** 0 to 3 s the cluttered "before"; 3 to 10 s the product action; 10 to 20 s the "after"; 20 to 25 s tagline and URL.
