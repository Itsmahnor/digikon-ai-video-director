**When to use:** reviewing or planning any launch or feature clip.

**Lessons:** never invent product UI labels; mirror the real app's copy exactly. Find the one moment only your product can deliver and give it the most screen time; the rest of the video sets it up. A before/after works better staggered: land the before first for about 0.7 to 1 second, then bring in the after with a quick, springy arrival, rather than showing both at once. End card: one headline and one CTA, using the real landing-page tagline, not a punchier invention. Ground the problem state in something viewers recognise without explanation.

**Check:** can a viewer who has never seen the product say what the problem was after three seconds?

**Claims:** only claims backed by facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials.

**Example:** the cluttered inbox (before) holds for one second; the clean inbox (after) lands with a short overshoot.
