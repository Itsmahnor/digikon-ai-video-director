**When to use:** before rendering, whenever the user has not said where the video will be published.

**Rule:** ask which platforms before rendering, because cropping a landscape cut to vertical afterwards rarely works. Typical sizes seen in practice: 1920x1080 for 16:9, 1080x1920 for 9:16, 1080x1350 for 4:5 feed, 1200x1200 for 1:1. Recommended pack: an MP4 in each requested aspect ratio, a GIF for embeds, caption variants per platform (length and tone differ), and a list version for text posts.

**Check first:** maximum durations and sizes change; confirm in the verified platform_specs before delivery.

**Example:** a 25-second launch clip delivered as 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 MP4s plus a GIF, each with captions re-checked for safe areas.

**Claims:** these sizes and limits are working defaults; do not present a platform's limit to the user as fact until it is confirmed.
