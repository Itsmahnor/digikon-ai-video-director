**When to use:** after a few self-iterations of any short video, before final render.

**Method:** render about 20 frames (the peak moments and transitions) and have four independent reviews, each producing a prioritised fix list: a design and layout critic (alignment, proportions, cropping), a text and readability critic (legibility at 720p, contrast, truncation, typos), a narrative and pacing critic (story arc, attention peaks, earned duration, CTA strength), and a brand consistency critic (fonts, colours, UI chrome matching the real product). Merge the lists with your own notes into one revision plan before rendering again.

**Why:** reviewers who did not build it catch what the builder has stopped seeing.

**Claims warning:** the brand critic also checks that no label, feature or number is invented.
