**When to use:** planning any short vertical video for a ranked feed.

**Idea:** the ranking is driven mostly by how much of the video is watched. A short video watched fully beats a long one watched halfway. Rewatches, shares and saves signal that the video was worth more than one pass; likes count for little; a fast swipe-away in the first two seconds is the strongest negative signal. The weights are reported by creators, not official, so treat them as heuristics.

**Design consequences:** keep it as short as the story allows; cut anything that does not move the story; put the payoff or the tension in frame one; avoid logos, greetings and slow zooms in the first second; make the text carry the promise for muted viewers.

**Claims warning:** do not promise reach or virality.

**Example:** a 15-second tip that shows the result first, then three steps, ending in a clean loop.
