**When to use:** short vertical videos where rewatches help.

**Method:** make the ending connect to the opening, by finishing a sentence that began the video, returning to the first frame, or holding the same colour and composition. Avoid a hard "thanks for watching" end card that signals the viewer to leave. Keep audio continuous across the loop point.

**Pacing:** a loop works best on short videos; for longer ones, end on the next open question instead.

**Caution:** a loop that confuses the viewer (an unfinished sentence with no payoff) can feel like a trick; the second pass should reward the viewer.

**Example:** a recipe clip ends on the plated dish, which is also the frame the next play opens on.
