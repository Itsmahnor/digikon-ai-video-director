**When to use:** reviewing any hook before approving the script.

**Warnings by formula:** a cold-open result must be real and visible in frame, or it reads as bait. A pattern interrupt needs a payoff in the middle, or viewers learn to swipe your next video fast. A number reveal must never be invented, because fact-checking in comments costs more than the hook earned. An open-loop question must be answered inside the video. A relatable call-out must be specific, not generic. A bold claim must be defensible and supported in the video.

**Rule:** for every hook, write the payoff line and the time it lands.

**Check:** read the hook and the end of the video side by side; do they keep the same promise?

**Claims:** only claims backed by facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials.
