**When to use:** any vertical short for TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

**Structure:** the hook is the first one to three seconds, and it has three layers that fire together: the spoken line, the first on-screen text, and the opening visual. The text and the spoken line should differ, because many viewers watch the first second muted, so the text must carry the promise on its own.

**Formulas to choose from:** cold-open result, pattern interrupt, specific-number reveal, open-loop question, relatable call-out, bold claim with no hedge; for longer or serial videos a listicle promise, a story in medias res, a tutorial cold start.

**Pacing:** a 3 to 7 word text card; the visual changes or moves within the first second.

**Claims warning:** a specific-number hook needs a supplied number and source.

**Example:** cold-open result: first frame is the finished dashboard, text "Invoices, done by Monday", voice starts the how.
