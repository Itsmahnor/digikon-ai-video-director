**Visual-style skill adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar "silkscreen-poster" style. Draft: review before use.**

**When to use:** Use when a film should reveal information the way a poster is printed: one layer, one beat.

**Method:** Use opaque flat inks, a limited palette by role: sky, far, mid, near and one bright accent. Values step from light far to dark near, so depth is value, never blur. Gradients are forbidden; use stepped bands.

**Rule:** Offset layers slightly so a crescent of bare paper shows where registration slips, and give fresh ink a brief wet sheen. The accent is rare: the sun, the one route or product, the key number.

**Caution:** Reveal one layer per beat to carry the story. Not halftone and not line work. Place any information in a dark near band so it stays readable. The brand rules and the user's supplied assets still win, and a style is a guide, not a template. Do not imitate a living artist or another brand's identity.
