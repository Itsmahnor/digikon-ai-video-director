**Style-selection skill adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar style index. Draft: review before use.**

**When to use:** the brief has a topic but no named look, and the Director must propose one or two styles for the storyboard.

**Method:** start from the content, not the taste. Real numbers point to a chart-led style, a physical object or system to a drawing or diagram style, software to a dark interface style, a short news item to a caption-first style, a catalogue of items to a pictogram style. Offer one safe choice and one bolder one, each with a one-line reason.

**Rule:** the user's named style, brand rules and supplied assets win. Check that the chosen look can be built with the renderer's allowed operations before proposing it; a style that needs unsupported effects is not offered.

**Caution:** never claim a style fits because of a demo film the Director has not seen. A style name is not evidence that a render is possible.
