**Visual-style skill adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar "iso-infographic" style. Draft: review before use.**

**When to use:** Use when a system, process or supply chain should be shown as a diagram that comes alive and opens up to show its insides.

**Method:** Use true isometric projection with no perspective and no outlines on solids; separate faces with three tones. Put the system on a floating board with a visible cross-section, on a pale ground with five to seven flat hues.

**Rule:** Colour codes meaning like a legend: one hue per material or role, never reused. When the story needs this one, desaturate everything else toward warm grey. Build numbers from icons so they add up, and keep labels and leader lines inside the world.

**Caution:** Not low-poly 3D and not a flat explainer. Counts and labels must come from supplied facts. The brand rules and the user's supplied assets still win, and a style is a guide, not a template. Do not imitate a living artist or another brand's identity.
