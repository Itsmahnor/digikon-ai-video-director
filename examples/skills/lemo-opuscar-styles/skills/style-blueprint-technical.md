**Visual-style skill adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar "blueprint" style. Draft: review before use.**

**When to use:** Use when an object, system or plan should be explained like an engineering drawing, with a dry, exact tone.

**Method:** Work on one sheet of cyanotype-blue paper with white line work only: outlines, centre lines, hatching, dimension lines and numbered callouts, plus a title block and a notes column. Draw parts in drafting order. Projection stays two-dimensional, orthographic or isometric.

**Rule:** Blue and white only. Allow exactly one other colour, used once, for a stamp that carries the verdict. The designed thing is the character, and notes can carry the personality.

**Caution:** Never render in 3D. Dimensions and part names on screen must come from supplied specs, not invention. The brand rules and the user's supplied assets still win, and a style is a guide, not a template. Do not imitate a living artist or another brand's identity.
