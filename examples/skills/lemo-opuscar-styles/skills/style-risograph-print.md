**Visual-style skill adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar "risograph" style. Draft: review before use.**

**When to use:** Use when a warm, tactile, printed look suits an indie, editorial or cultural film.

**Method:** Limit the palette to two or three translucent spot inks on warm off-white paper. Other colours come only from overprint (inks multiply) or tints. Use halftone dots for mid-tones, each ink on its own screen angle.

**Rule:** Offset every ink layer by a few pixels so edges show slivers of paper. Add ink grain and light pinholes, with no smooth gradients; use stepped halftone instead. Paper white is a colour: use it for highlights and subtitles.

**Caution:** Choose inks whose pairs mix into useful colours and keep at least one that reads as a silhouette alone. Keep small text crisp rather than textured. The brand rules and the user's supplied assets still win, and a style is a guide, not a template. Do not imitate a living artist or another brand's identity.
