**Visual-style skill adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar "swiss-motion" style. Draft: review before use.**

**When to use:** Use when a film needs an exact, rule-driven typographic look: a rigorous explainer, a manifesto or a data-light brand piece.

**Method:** Lay everything on a modular grid with generous margins and a baseline. Use one typeface, black and light grey, and one saturated signal colour. Use extreme scale contrast, such as a giant numeral beside small notes.

**Rule:** Every element snaps to the grid and every move lands on a beat. Nothing bounces, flies or spins.

**Caution:** Not a keynote: no gradients, glow or device renders. Keep the signal colour for the one thing that matters. Build it as flat shapes and text in Remotion or Hyperframes. The brand rules and the user's supplied assets still win, and a style is a guide, not a template. Do not imitate a living artist or another brand's identity.
