**Visual-style skill adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar "whiteboard" style. Draft: review before use.**

**When to use:** Use when a concept needs a friendly, teach-it-live explainer on one board.

**Method:** Treat the board as a map: each idea lives in its own region and the camera travels between regions, so distance on the board means distance in the argument. Write text stroke by stroke in real order, never as animated blocks.

**Rule:** Use at most three marker colours, each with one meaning held for the whole film: structure, process or measurement, and the thing that matters. No hands; markers, magnets and the eraser move on their own. Let each stroke have a sound.

**Caution:** Not a kinetic-type explainer and not a children's story look. Keep handwriting legible at phone size and use short phrases. The brand rules and the user's supplied assets still win, and a style is a guide, not a template. Do not imitate a living artist or another brand's identity.
