**Visual-style skill adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar "caption-news" style. Draft: review before use.**

**When to use:** Use when a news or explainer short must read with the sound off, built on real footage and a source document.

**Method:** Make the caption the shot: one short line set huge, slammed in two or three chunks, with one keyword on a coloured block and a smaller translated line under it. Choose footage that sits under the line.

**Rule:** Show evidence on screen: each number arrives with a source tag in the same shot, and key figures are first shown highlighted in the original document. Open with the largest number in the first moment, then answer it with one fact that changes its meaning. Cut on a fixed beat grid.

**Caution:** Claims: never show a number without its source, and never caption something the footage or document does not support. Keep footage credits visible. The brand rules and the user's supplied assets still win, and a style is a guide, not a template. Do not imitate a living artist or another brand's identity.
