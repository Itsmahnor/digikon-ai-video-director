**Visual-style skill adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar "dark-keynote" style. Draft: review before use.**

**When to use:** Use when a software product launch needs a premium look where the interface is the protagonist.

**Method:** Use a near-black stage with a cool neutral ladder, a hairline grid, soft light and one luminous brand accent. Show windows, tiles, notifications and one giant number moving with snap-grid precision.

**Rule:** Reserve the accent for what the product is or does: the hero element, the call to action, the key number. A warning colour may appear only while the problem exists and must not return after the turn.

**Caution:** Use the story shape of disorder, one decisive action, then empty space. Only show interface content the user supplied; sample text must not state results. Not a physical product render and not a screencast. The brand rules and the user's supplied assets still win, and a style is a guide, not a template. Do not imitate a living artist or another brand's identity.
