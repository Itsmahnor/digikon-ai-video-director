**Visual-style skill adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar "pictogram-motion" style. Draft: review before use.**

**When to use:** Use when a catalogue of items or a simple story should move fast on the beat with bold, friendly graphics.

**Method:** Build everything from one geometry: round-capped bars, discs, half-discs, rings and capsules. Use a square-grid background of discs in five tones of one hue, with whole rows sliding sideways to drive backgrounds and transitions.

**Rule:** Make type architectural: a very heavy title, a heavy second-language line, small mono meta text in the corners. One hue per chapter. Light hues take dark type, and dark hues take cream.

**Caution:** Use it as a catalogue, one card at a time on the beat, or as a pictogram story with one hero and a turn. Keep the pattern at low contrast under type. The brand rules and the user's supplied assets still win, and a style is a guide, not a template. Do not imitate a living artist or another brand's identity.
