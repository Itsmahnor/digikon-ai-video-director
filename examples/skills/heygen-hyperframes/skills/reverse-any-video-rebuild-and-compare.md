**When to use:** the user wants an editable recreation of an existing video.

**Method:** the original decides the frame: match its exact size, frame rate and duration, find every cut (including jump cuts and punch-ins), keep its soundtrack, and read consecutive frames wherever something changes. Rebuild components (text, UI, charts) in the project, cut sub-assets (photos, logos) from the original, and verify the render against the original at sampled frames.

**Caution:** only recreate videos the user owns or has the right to reuse.
