**When to use:** composing any frame that will carry captions.

**Rule:** the caption is a small, minimal line composited over the whole film, near the bottom of the canvas (roughly the bottom 5 to 8 percent). Centre the composition on the true vertical centre of the frame; do not shift content up to make room, and do not leave a dead band. Content may run to the canvas bottom.

**Courtesy:** do not park critical small text (a URL, a legal line) in the bottom centre where the caption line sits. Large imagery under captions is fine; judge legibility on real frames, not with a keep-out rule.

**When captions are off:** the same layout freedom applies; the overlay simply does not exist.

**With the caption model:** the verbatim rail rides in front, a rare embed sits behind the subject.
