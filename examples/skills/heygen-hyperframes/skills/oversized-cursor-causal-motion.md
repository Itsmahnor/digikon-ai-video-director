**When to use:** UI demos and scenes that feel static or stale.

**Rule:** a deliberately large pointer enters, walks the viewer's eye to the next point of interest, clicks to cause what happens next, and leaves. It is cheap (one element, transform-only) and gives causal motion: the click is why the morph or transition happens. Make it large (an actual-size cursor vanishes at video scale), keep one look per film, and match a recognisable brand pointer when the product has one.

**Example:** cursor travels to "Send reminder", clicks, and the invoice row morphs into its paid state.

**Caution:** only imitate a brand's cursor look when it is the subject of the video.
