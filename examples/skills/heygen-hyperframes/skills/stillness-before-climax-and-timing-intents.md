**When to use:** setting durations for entrances, exits and staggers.

**Rules:** schedule a 0.3 to 0.75 second pause between the major action and its result, the dramatic comma. A single entry takes about 0.8 s at most; a longer build-up is a multi-element stagger, not one slow element. Exit is about 75% of entry (the cut-the-curve seam inverts this). Total stagger at most about 0.5 s; with eight or more elements tighten the per-item delay. Do not use bouncing or elastic eases; a small entry overshoot is fine. Similar elements share one ease and duration. Use only two or three inter-scene transitions in a film and repeat them.

**Example:** the total on a pricing card lands, holds 0.5 s, then the savings badge appears.
