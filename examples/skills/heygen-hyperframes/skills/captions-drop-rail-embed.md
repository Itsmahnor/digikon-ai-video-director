**When to use:** captioning talking-head, launch or explainer video.

**Model:** each spoken phrase is dropped (filler, stutters), set on the rail (the default: a clean, readable verbatim subtitle composited over the film, with an optional emphasis on a punch word), or embedded (a scarce promoted peak: one big word placed behind the subject). Embed at most once per beat, never two close together; a short clip usually has one.

**Rule:** captions are an overlay, never a reserved band you shift content up to avoid.

**Caution:** embedding every word is the common mistake. For voice-over explainers, words must read, so keep the rail.
