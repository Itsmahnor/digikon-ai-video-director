**When to use:** inside a scene, with no cut.

**Waterfall entry:** title cards, segment openers and list intros arrive as a staggered cascade rising from below, one element after another, instead of everything at once.

**Nudge curve:** when a composed group must make room, slide the whole group with a three-phase slow-fast-slow move rather than a linear shift.

**Pacing:** keep the stagger short enough that the last element lands while the first is still readable.

**Example:** four feature bullets cascade in at about 80 ms apart; the group then nudges left to reveal a screenshot.
