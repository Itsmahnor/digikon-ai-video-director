**When to use:** a scene finishes its entrance with seconds left.

**Rule:** idle sine loops (breathe, float, drift, glow pulse) are not allowed as sustained motion; they read as the video waiting. A scene that finishes entering with time left is a planning bug: add story, not wobble. Name one route for every phase between entry and exit: staged reveals (hold content back and pay it off on narration beats), camera with intent (establish wide, travel, arrive on the subject), sequenced UI life (progress advances, highlights step, counts tick), animated sequences (a card files into a stack, a result assembles), or cursor-led action.

**Test:** pause at any second; something meaningful must be mid-flight, such as a reveal landing, the camera travelling or the UI doing what the narration says.
