**When to use:** the user asks for something "in the style of" a known explainer genre (collage explainer, finance explainer, animated science explainer, brand 3D explainer).

**Rule:** pick the style the user named, keep its recurring grammar (typography, palette, motion language, pacing), and apply it to the user's topic and facts. If the user supplies one video to copy, that is a reverse-engineering task, not style sampling.

**Caution:** copy a style's grammar, never another creator's footage, characters or brand marks.
