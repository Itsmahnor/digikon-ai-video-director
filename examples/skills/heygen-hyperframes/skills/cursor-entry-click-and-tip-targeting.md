**When to use:** any scene with a pointer-led action.

**Entry:** the cursor always enters from off-screen (canonically from below), in one decelerating glide of roughly 0.4 to 0.9 s. Never fade it in at a resting position or reveal it with a mask; that reads as a glitch. Default to a straight path along one axis unless the diagonal is the story.

**Targeting:** the hot spot is the arrow tip, not the box centre. Land the tip on the target's centre and pivot press scaling on the tip.

**Click:** an asymmetric compress and expand (the press about half as long as the release) reads as a real tap. Keep one cursor look per film, large at video scale, with a soft shadow, above all scene content.

**Caution:** copy a brand's pointer only when the product is the subject and the pointer is recognisable.
