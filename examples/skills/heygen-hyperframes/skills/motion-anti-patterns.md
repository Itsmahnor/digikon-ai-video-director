**When to use:** reviewing motion in a draft.

**Replace:** scene entrances authored in isolation, with a ledger written first; crossfades between scenes, with a velocity-matched cut in the film's current direction; an exit that completes and then the scene changes, with a cut mid-motion on both sides; an entry that starts from rest after a cut, with an entry already at least half way along its path; a receding exit followed by a grow-from-small entry, with matched scale-velocity sign; idle wobble to fill time, with a purposeful motion route; a direction flip without a cause, with a visible force or the current direction; reserved vectors used for variety, with meaningful use only; a reaction a few frames after its cause, with same-frame ignition; action jumping straight to result, with a short stillness first.

**Use:** a checklist at review time, written as pass or fail per item with the scene named.

**Rule:** treat each row as a pass or fail check at review time, and write down which scene failed it.
