**When to use:** a new film of more than about 15 seconds that tells something.

**Flow:** read the video type's guidance (launch, explainer, music, data, vertical, 3D, character); write three storylines as three different films in words only and let the person pick unless they asked for no stops; write a spec; build; run the checker and fix every error and weigh every warning; take review snapshots and write a review note; render only after the person approves the preview, asks for the file or asks for no stops, and after the check and review pass.

**Rules:** build what was asked, with no voice-over, captions or new aspect ratio unless the request calls for them; a film over about 15 s that tells something gets music and sound on its visible actions. For an existing project, look at what plays when and what the person changed since your last turn, and build on it.
