**When to use:** choosing how one scene hands over to the next.

**Catalog:** zoom-through (go deeper within a thought); inverse zoom-through (an arrival or payoff beat); cut the curve (the default boundary, using partial travel of roughly a tenth of the frame with mirrored strong eases); waterfall cut (word-by-word handoff between big-text beats); rack-focus blur cut (the one cut you want seen, like a camera focus pull).

**Rule:** pick by meaning, not novelty. Default to cut the curve; spend the others where the story earns them. The incoming scene's own entrances must not fight the seam direction during the seam window.

**Example:** headline scene to feature scene uses cut the curve; the final price reveal uses inverse zoom-through.
