**General visual-design skill (not video-specific).**

**When to use:** a brand frame, poster-style end card or title design needs a distinct look, not a template.

**Method:** two steps. First name the look (one or two words) and write a short philosophy of how it shows up in space and form, colour and material, scale and rhythm. Then express it visually, with the design carrying most of the meaning and text kept to a minimum accent.

**Rule:** the philosophy guides; the brand rules and the user's supplied assets still win.

**Caution:** do not imitate a living artist or another brand's identity.
