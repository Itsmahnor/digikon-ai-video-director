**When to use:** adding titles, lower thirds, callouts or captions to narrated video.

**Structure:** add a graphic only to show what the camera cannot (a number, a comparison, a structure); if it can be removed without loss, remove it. Do not echo the voice in text; use on-screen text for what the voice leaves out, such as a name, a figure or a term to remember. A graphic enters shortly before the word that introduces it, at most half a second early, and shows content within its first second; an empty graphic waiting for the voice reads as a mistake. Keep text inside platform safe zones and large enough for a phone.

**Example:** a lower third appears 0.3 s before the speaker names the product and holds for the length of the sentence.

**Check:** play the cut with the sound off; every graphic should still make sense, and none should appear before there is something to read.
