**When to use:** adding titles, lower thirds, callouts or captions to narrated video.

**Structure:** add a graphic only to show what the camera cannot (a number, a comparison, a structure); if it can be removed without loss, remove it. Do not repeat the voice in text; put on screen what is not said (a name, a number, a key term). A graphic enters shortly before the word that introduces it, at most half a second early, and shows content within its first second; an empty graphic waiting for the voice reads as a mistake. Keep text inside platform safe zones and large enough for a phone.

**Example:** a lower third appears 0.3 s before the speaker names the product and holds for the length of the sentence.
