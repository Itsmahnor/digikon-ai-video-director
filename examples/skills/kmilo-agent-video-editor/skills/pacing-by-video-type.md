**When to use:** setting the cut rhythm for a given format.

**Guide (creator and house practice, not official):** talking head, a change every 2 to 5 seconds using jump cuts and punch-ins; explainer, a first interrupt around 25 to 35 seconds then a refresh every minute or two, with a small hook every 30 to 90 seconds; screen tutorial, change when the action changes, with shots of at least about 5 seconds and a hard cut within a step; podcast or interview, cut on speaker changes and reactions rather than on a timer; short vertical, about 2 to 3 seconds per shot with something new every few seconds.

**Floor for text:** a shot carrying text never ends before the text can be read; use max(1.5 s, words x 0.33 s + 0.5 s).

**Caution:** figures from blogs and tools are ranges, not law; where no serious source exists, decide from the content and say so.
