**When to use:** at the mix stage of any video with speech and music.

**Structure:** master to about -14 LUFS integrated with true peak at or below -1 dBTP for video platforms. Keep background music well under the voice (a gap of roughly 18 to 25 dB is common practice), duck it 6 to 12 dB while the voice speaks, with a fast attack and slower release. Keep sound effects 12 to 24 dB under the voice. Clean dialogue gently; over-reduced noise sounds underwater.

**Claims warning:** platform loudness behaviour is not always published; these are working targets, not guarantees.

**Example:** voice at -16 LUFS, music bed at about -38 LUFS, ducked a further 8 dB under speech.
