**When to use:** YouTube videos and Shorts, especially tutorials and talking heads.

**Structure:** show or state what the title and thumbnail promise within the first seconds: the result, the most striking moment or the question being answered. No logo intro, no greeting, no "in this video we will". Be concrete about who it is for and what they get. After the hook, renew the image at the pace of the video type, use open loops ("the third one surprised me") and pay them off, and cut tangents, not only pauses.

**Pacing:** for Shorts the first three seconds decide; for long form the first 30 seconds are the intro that analytics measure.

**Claims warning:** a hook that over-promises hurts retention; only promise what the video delivers.

**Example:** tutorial opens on the finished result for three seconds, then restarts at step one.
