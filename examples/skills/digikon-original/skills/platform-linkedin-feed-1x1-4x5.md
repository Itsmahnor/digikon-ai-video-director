**When to use:** professional and feed placements.

**Rules:** square (1:1) or 4:5 takes more feed space on mobile; assume sound off, so burn captions in; lead with the value in the first seconds; keep it short, usually 15 to 30 seconds; a professional tone and plain language.

**Pacing:** calm and clear; text large; brand mark steady.

**Check first:** confirm current length and format limits in the verified platform_specs.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. B2B claims about ROI or savings need a supplied source.

**Example:** a 20 s 4:5 feature clip with captions and a soft CTA.

**Review checklist:** the first line of text is legible without sound; the logo appears but does not dominate; the CTA points to one destination; the aspect ratio is chosen for the placement, not defaulted; claims about results are phrased with their source.
