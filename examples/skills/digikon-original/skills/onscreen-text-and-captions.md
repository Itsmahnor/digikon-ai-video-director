**When to use:** any video; most feeds start muted.

**Rules:** burn captions in for social; at most two short lines at once; keep to roughly 3 to 7 words on hook cards; high contrast (text on a solid or scrimmed area, never directly on busy footage); large enough to read on a phone at arm's length; consistent font, case and position through the film; keep text inside platform safe zones.

**Do not** repeat on screen what the voice says word for word, except as captions. Show what is not said: a number, a name, a term.

**Timing:** text appears when the word is spoken and stays until it is read.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review.

**Example:** a spoken line "Send in three clicks" with a card "3 clicks" and captions below.
