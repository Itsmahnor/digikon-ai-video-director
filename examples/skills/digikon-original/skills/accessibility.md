**When to use:** every video.

**Rules:** captions for all speech, synchronised and accurate; text contrast high and readable at small sizes; do not rely on colour alone to carry meaning; avoid flashing more than three times a second; keep motion gentle for looped or background video and respect reduced-motion preferences on web; consider an audio description or a descriptive transcript where the picture carries information not in the voice; provide a transcript when the video is hosted on a page.

**Check:** play it muted; play it on a phone; read every card at normal speed.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review.

**Example:** a muted playthrough that still delivers the message because captions and key text carry it.

**Rule:** accessibility is not a final polish step. Decide caption style, contrast and motion limits at the storyboard stage so they do not force a redesign later.
