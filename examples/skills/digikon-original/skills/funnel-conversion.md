**When to use:** the viewer is ready to decide.

**What to say:** the offer, the next step and the reassurance (guarantee, trial, returns) if real, with one clear CTA.

**Format:** 10 to 20 seconds, direct; retargeting or product page placements.

**Pacing:** quick and plain; the CTA held long enough to read twice.

**Metrics to expect:** click-through, conversion rate.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. Deadlines, discounts, guarantees and trial terms must be real and supplied.

**Example:** "Start your 14-day trial" only if a 14-day trial exists.

**Handoff:** keep the conversion video consistent with the page it links to: the same offer wording, price and visual identity, so the viewer does not feel a mismatch on arrival. Ask the user for the exact landing page and the offer terms before scripting, and re-check them if the offer changes.
