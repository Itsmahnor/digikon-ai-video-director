**When to use:** a local business (cleaning, repair, salon, clinic admin, trades) wants calls or bookings.

**Structure:** the local problem in the owner's words, the service in action, one proof, one way to book.

**Scene beats (20 s):** 0-3 s the problem in a recognisable local setting; 3-11 s the service being done; 11-16 s proof (a real review or a before/after); 16-20 s area served, phone or booking link.

**Pacing:** plain and quick; the contact route stays on screen for at least 3 seconds.

**Asset needs:** real photos or footage of the work, area served, phone or link, opening hours, any licence or insurance wording the user wants shown.

**Claims:** only claims backed by facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims for review. Reviews and ratings must be real and attributable; do not claim licences, insurance or guarantees that were not given.

**Example:** a cleaning service shows a real before/after of one room and the booking number.
