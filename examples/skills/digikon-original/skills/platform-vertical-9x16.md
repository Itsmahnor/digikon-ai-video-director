**When to use:** any vertical short.

**Rules:** 9:16; hook in the first 1 to 3 seconds (spoken line, text and visual together); captions on because many viewers start muted; keep key text and faces away from the top and bottom areas covered by interface elements; keep to the shorter lengths (about 15 to 30 seconds) unless the story needs more.

**Pacing:** a new visual or beat every 2 to 4 seconds.

**Check first:** the exact interface safe zones and length limits change; confirm with the verified platform_specs.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review.

**Example:** 20 s clip with captions in the middle third and the CTA above the lower interface band.
