**When to use:** an upcoming event or launch needs attention before the date.

**Structure:** a hint at the experience, the date and place, who it is for, the way to register.

**Scene beats (15 s):** 0-3 s striking visual and hook; 3-10 s glimpses, fast cuts to music; 10-13 s date, time and place as large text; 13-15 s CTA and URL.

**Pacing:** high energy, cuts on the beat, but the details hold for at least 2 seconds.

**Asset needs:** exact date, time, venue, registration link, speaker or guest names (with permission), visuals.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. Do not confirm speakers, guests or details that are not confirmed; write "to be announced" where applicable.

**Example:** a product launch webinar: three screen glimpses, "12 June, 3 pm", register link.
