**When to use:** any ad, creator video, testimonial, giveaway or video using generated people or voices.

**Rules:** label paid partnerships and promotional content using the platform's disclosure tools and plain on-screen wording where required; never present a paid or gifted opinion as spontaneous; never present a synthetic person or voice as a real customer; get written permission for people who appear and for any quoted testimonial; keep the wording required by the user's legal team for regulated products.

**Ask the user:** whether the content is paid, gifted or organic; whether a real person or a synthetic one is used; and which legal wording is required.

**Caution:** the rules differ by country and platform; treat this as a reminder to check, not as legal advice.

**Claims:** only claims backed by facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims for review.

**Example:** a creator video for a product sent free carries the platform's paid-partnership label and a spoken "gifted" line.

**Check:** before approval, list every person, voice and gifted product in the video and write beside each whether it needs a label or a release.
