**When to use:** introducing a range, a season or a themed set of products.

**Structure:** the theme in one line, a rhythmic run of product looks, a detail or two, the range name and where to see it.

**Scene beats (20 s):** 0-3 s the theme and the strongest look; 3-15 s five to eight looks of about 2 s each, cut on a steady rhythm; 15-18 s details or colours; 18-20 s name, URL and logo.

**Pacing:** consistent rhythm; consistent framing and lighting between looks.

**Asset needs:** photos or footage of each product in the same style, exact names, the range link.

**Claims:** only claims backed by facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims for review. Show colours and materials truthfully; do not show products that are not in the range.

**Example:** a spring collection of six pieces, one look each, ending on the collection page URL.
