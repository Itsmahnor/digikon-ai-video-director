**When to use:** the viewer does not know the brand or may not know they have the problem.

**What to say:** name the problem or desire in the viewer's words, show one surprising or relatable moment, introduce the brand lightly. Do not push a purchase.

**Format:** 15 to 30 seconds, hook-led, vertical for social, captions on, brand mark present but small.

**Pacing:** fast, one idea.

**Metrics to expect:** reach, hook rate and watch time, not sales.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review.

**Example:** a relatable scene about late payments with the product appearing in the last third.

**Handoff:** end on a reason to look closer (a follow, a short link or a retargetable view) rather than a hard sell, and keep the same visual identity as the consideration-stage videos so the viewer recognises the brand on the next touch. Ask the user which audience this top-of-funnel video should reach, because the hook depends on it.
