**When to use:** news that people should hear (new location, partnership, milestone, hours).

**Structure:** the news in the first line, why it matters to the viewer, what to do.

**Scene beats (15-30 s):** 0-4 s the headline; 4-18 s the key details with visuals; 18-25 s what changes for the viewer; 25-30 s CTA.

**Pacing:** clean and direct; short sentences; one idea per card.

**Asset needs:** the approved announcement text, logo, any photos, link.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. Use the approved wording; do not enlarge a milestone ("our biggest ever") unless supplied.

**Example:** "We are moving to a new studio" with the new address and opening date.

**Check:** the first line must say what changed and when; if the news needs a second sentence to be understood, rewrite the first.
