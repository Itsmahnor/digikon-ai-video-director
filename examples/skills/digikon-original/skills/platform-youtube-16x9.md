**When to use:** landscape videos for YouTube, websites and presentations.

**Rules:** 16:9; deliver the promise in the first seconds (no logo intro); make the thumbnail and title match what the video delivers; chapters help longer videos; for pre-roll, design the first 5 seconds to work before a skip.

**Pacing:** slightly more room than vertical, but renew the picture regularly.

**Check first:** ad lengths and formats change; confirm in the verified platform_specs.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review.

**Example:** a 90 s demo with a result-first cold open and chapters.

**Review checklist:** the first five seconds work without context; the title promise appears on screen early; captions or a transcript are available; end screens or a next-step card are planned in the last 10 to 20 seconds; the thumbnail is designed with the video, not afterwards.
