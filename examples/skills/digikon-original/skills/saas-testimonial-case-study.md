**When to use:** a prospect needs proof from someone like them.

**Structure:** the customer and their situation, the problem, the change they made, the result in their own words, the CTA.

**Scene beats (60-90 s):** 0-6 s a one-line quote or result as the hook; 6-25 s situation and problem; 25-55 s using the product; 55-80 s result; 80-90 s CTA.

**Pacing:** let the speaker finish sentences; cut on natural pauses; captions on.

**Asset needs:** a real customer interview or written, approved quote, logo permission, product footage.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. Quotes and figures must be provided and approved by the customer; never write a testimonial or a number for them. If none exists, say so instead of inventing one.

**Example:** "Our collections time dropped" only if the customer said it and approved the claim.
