**When to use:** the shopper needs to see the product clearly and want it.

**Structure:** hero shot, three key angles or features, scale or in-use context, price or offer, CTA.

**Scene beats (20 s):** 0-3 s hero close-up with the benefit as text; 3-14 s three features each with a short label; 14-17 s in-use context; 17-20 s CTA and price if supplied.

**Pacing:** 1 to 3 second shots; one clean background; consistent lighting and colour.

**Asset needs:** product photos or footage, true colours, packaging if shown, logo, price and link.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. Never show features, materials, sizes or certifications that are not on the product page.

**Example:** a water bottle: hero spin, insulated lid label, pour shot, in-bag shot, CTA.
