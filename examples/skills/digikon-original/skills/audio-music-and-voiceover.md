**When to use:** deciding sound for a video.

**Rules:** speech is the priority; music sits well below the voice and is ducked while someone speaks; use music and sound effects only with a recorded licence that allows the use; keep a mix quiet enough to understand on a phone speaker; match music energy to the funnel stage; voice-over needs a clean recording or an approved synthetic voice, and a synthetic voice must be disclosed to the user as such; many feeds start muted, so the film must work with captions alone.

**Ask the user:** whether they want music or voice-over, the voice, and the music licence.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review.

**Example:** a calm bed under the narrator for an explainer, dropping 8 dB when the narrator speaks.

**Check:** play the mix on a phone speaker and with the sound off; the film must work in both.
