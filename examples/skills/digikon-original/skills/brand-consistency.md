**When to use:** every video that carries a brand.

**Rules:** use the brand's supplied colours, fonts and logo files; one palette (primary, accent, neutrals) and one or two typefaces; keep the logo clear space and minimum size; place the logo consistently, for example the end card and a small corner mark; do not stretch, recolour or add effects to the logo; ensure contrast between text and background.

**If missing:** ask for the logo, colours and fonts rather than inventing them; if the user has none, propose a neutral palette and say it is a placeholder.

**Check:** the same colour values across every scene; no scene introduces a new font.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review.

**Example:** brand navy and one orange accent, one sans-serif family, logo on the end card and a small corner mark.
