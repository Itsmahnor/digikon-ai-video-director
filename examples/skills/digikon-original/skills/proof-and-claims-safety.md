**When to use:** any time a statement about the product, price, results or people is written.

**Rules:** every factual claim traces to a fact the user supplied (a document, page, figure or approved quote). If it does not, remove it, rephrase it as a feeling or ask. Never create statistics, prices, discounts, awards, customer names or testimonials. Do not imply endorsements or comparisons that are not supported.

**Flag for review:** health and medical, financial returns, legal outcomes, children, environmental claims, "best", "guaranteed", "clinically proven", and before/after results.

**Report instead of invent:** when a claim is wanted but unsupported, list it as a missing input so the user can supply the source.

**Example:** the user wants "saves 10 hours a week"; no source is supplied; use "less time on invoicing" or ask for the source.
