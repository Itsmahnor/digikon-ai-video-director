**When to use:** a paid or organic ad for any service where the offer is simple.

**Structure:** hook, one benefit, proof if supplied, CTA.

**Scene beats (15 s):** 0-3 s hook; 3-9 s the benefit demonstrated; 9-12 s proof; 12-15 s CTA and contact.

**Pacing:** fast, with text readable in the first second; captions on.

**Asset needs:** a clear single offer, logo, service photos or footage, phone or URL.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. If no proof is supplied, omit the proof beat and lengthen the benefit beat.

**Example:** a local cleaning service: before/after room, "Book a clean this week", phone number.

**Review checklist:** the offer is clear in the first three seconds; there is exactly one CTA and one contact route; captions are on; the footage shows the real service or location; the ad would still make sense with the sound off.
