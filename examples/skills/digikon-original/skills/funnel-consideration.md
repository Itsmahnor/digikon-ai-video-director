**When to use:** the viewer knows the problem and is comparing options.

**What to say:** how the product solves it, why it differs, proof, and answers to the top objections.

**Format:** 45 to 90 seconds, demo or explainer or testimonial; landscape or square is fine for these placements.

**Pacing:** steady; allow time to read the UI; chapters if long.

**Metrics to expect:** watch time, click-through to the product page.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. Differentiation claims need support; objections must be answered with true statements.

**Example:** a demo that ends with the pricing page link.

**Handoff:** each consideration video should answer one objection and point to one next step, such as a demo, a comparison or a pricing page. Ask the user for the top three objections they hear from prospects, and build one video per objection instead of one long video that answers all of them.
