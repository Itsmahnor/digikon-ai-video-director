**When to use:** before planning, whenever the brief lacks something a scene needs.

**Check and ask:** logo (file, clear-space rules); brand colours and fonts; product footage or screenshots (real, current, permission to show); customer quotes (approved); prices, offers, deadlines and terms; the URL, phone number or code for the CTA; music or voice licences; the platform and length; legal wording; people on screen and releases.

**Rule:** report the gap as a missing input with the scene that needs it; do not substitute stock footage, invented logos, placeholder statistics or invented quotes without telling the user.

**Output:** a short list "Needed from you" ordered by how many scenes are blocked.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review.

**Example:** "Scene 3 needs real dashboard footage; scene 5 needs the CTA URL; the logo file is missing."
