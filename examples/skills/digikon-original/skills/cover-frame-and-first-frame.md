**When to use:** every video that will appear in a grid, feed or search result.

**Rules:** the cover frame should show the payoff or the subject clearly, with a few words of large text, and still read at thumbnail size. The first frame of the video should carry motion, the result or the tension, never a logo or a blank. If the platform lets you pick a cover, pick it; if it uses the first frame, design that frame as the cover.

**Check:** shrink the frame to a small square; can you still tell what the video is about and read the text?

**Caution:** the thumbnail must match what the video delivers; a misleading cover loses the viewer and trust.

**Claims:** only claims backed by facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims for review.

**Example:** a before/after split with the words "Paid in 2 days" only if that outcome was supplied.
