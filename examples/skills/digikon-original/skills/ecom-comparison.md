**When to use:** the shopper is choosing between your product and an alternative or between your own models.

**Structure:** the decision criteria, side-by-side on each, a fair verdict, CTA.

**Scene beats (30-45 s):** 0-4 s the question; each criterion 6-8 s with a split screen; 4 s verdict; 4 s CTA.

**Pacing:** same time for each side; clear labels; do not rush the verdict.

**Asset needs:** verified specs for both sides, permission to show other brands, product footage.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. Compare only on facts that can be verified; avoid disparaging competitors; show a competitor's logo or product only when permitted. Comparative superiority claims need evidence.

**Example:** two of your own models compared on weight, battery and price from the spec sheet.

**Check:** show the comparison to someone unfamiliar with the products; they should be able to say which model suits which person without being told.
