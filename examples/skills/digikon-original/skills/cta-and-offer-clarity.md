**When to use:** the last beat of every video and any video that states an offer.

**Rules:** one CTA per video; it names the action and the place ("Start your free trial at example.com"); the offer states what, how much, until when, and any condition; the CTA is spoken, shown as text and carries the logo; hold it at least 2 seconds.

**Do not** stack several CTAs, hide terms in tiny text, or promise outcomes.

**Ask the user:** the exact offer, price, deadline, URL or phone number, and any required legal wording.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. Never invent a discount, deadline, trial length or guarantee.

**Example:** "Book a free 15-minute call: example.com/call" with the URL on screen and in the voice-over.

**Check:** cover the logo and read only the last card; can a stranger say what to do, where, and by when?
