**When to use:** the same story must run at several lengths or placements.

**Method:** edit the full version first; make shorter versions by removing whole beats, not by speeding up. A typical set: a 30-second master, a 15-second cutdown (hook, one benefit, CTA), and a 6-second bumper (one visual, one line, logo). Keep the hook and the CTA in every version; protect the one proof beat in versions of 15 seconds or more.

**Versions:** vary the aspect ratio by re-framing, not by cropping blindly; check captions and safe areas in each. Name files by length, ratio and version.

**Check:** play each cutdown on its own; does it still make sense with no context?

**Claims:** only claims backed by facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims for review.

**Example:** 30 s master (problem, demo, proof, CTA), 15 s (hook, demo, CTA), 6 s (result shot, line, logo).
