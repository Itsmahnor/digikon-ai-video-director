**When to use:** one common customer question deserves a clear 30 to 60 second answer.

**Structure:** the question as the hook, the answer in one sentence, a short demonstration of the steps on the real product, and where to find more help.

**Scene beats (45 s):** 0-4 s the question in text and voice; 4-10 s the one-sentence answer; 10-38 s the steps on screen, one per 8 to 10 seconds; 38-45 s the link to the help page.

**Pacing:** calm and clear; hold each screen long enough to read; captions on.

**Asset needs:** a current screen recording, the exact steps from the support team, the help page link.

**Claims:** only claims backed by facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims for review. Describe only steps that exist in the current product; ask the user to confirm after any release.

**Example:** "How do I export my invoices?" with the three real clicks.
