**When to use:** new users need to reach their first success quickly.

**Structure:** the first success defined up front, then the shortest path to it, one screen per step, and what to do next.

**Scene beats (60-90 s):** 0-5 s the goal ("by the end you will have sent your first invoice"); one step per 10-15 s, each with a numbered label; final 10 s the success state and the next resource.

**Pacing:** slow and clear; hold each screen; repeat the control name when it is clicked.

**Asset needs:** a clean demo account recording, correct current UI, step list from the user.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. Do not describe steps that differ from the product's real flow; ask for the current flow if unsure.

**Example:** four numbered steps from sign-up to the first invoice, ending on the dashboard.
