**When to use:** the story depends on a number, a trend or a comparison.

**Rules:** one chart, one message per scene. State the takeaway in a title ("Invoices paid sooner"), then reveal the data in order: axis, then values, then the highlight. Start axes at zero for bars; label the unit and the period; keep the colour for the highlighted value consistent with the brand. Count-up numbers should reach the real value and settle; a number animating to a rounded value that differs from the source is a misstatement.

**Pacing:** reveal over 1.5 to 3 seconds and hold for reading time (at least about 2.5 s for a short label and number).

**Claims:** the data, source, period and unit must come from the user; show the source in small text where the platform allows; never invent, smooth or extrapolate data.

**Example:** a three-bar comparison of days-to-payment with the improvement highlighted, from the user's own report.
