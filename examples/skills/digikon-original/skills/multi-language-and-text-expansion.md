**When to use:** the video will be localised or the audience reads another language.

**Rules:** leave room for text to grow; translated text is often longer, so avoid fixed-width cards and tight layouts. Use fonts that cover the target scripts and test them. For right-to-left languages, mirror the layout and the direction of reading and motion where it carries meaning, and check punctuation and numbers. Keep captions in the viewer's language, with the voice-over language labelled. Have a native speaker review the translation of claims, prices and legal wording.

**Check:** render a still of the longest line in each language at the narrowest aspect ratio.

**Caution:** do not machine-translate legal or regulated claims without review.

**Claims:** only claims backed by facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims for review.
