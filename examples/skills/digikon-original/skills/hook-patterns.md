**When to use:** every video, at the start of scene design.

**Patterns (pick one and name it):** result first (open on the end state); problem in their words; pattern interrupt (unexpected action or visual); open-loop question; specific, supplied number; bold claim that the video then shows; relatable call-out ("If you run a small shop...").

**Rules:** spoken line, on-screen text and opening visual all work together and the text carries the message with the sound off. Product or result is visible within the first 3 seconds. No logo sting, greeting or "in this video".

**Pacing:** one idea in 3 seconds; the first cut or movement within 1 second.

**Test:** write three hooks for every video; they are the cheapest thing to vary.

**Claims:** only claims backed by the facts the user supplied; no invented statistics, prices, customer names or testimonials; flag regulated claims (health, finance, legal) for review. A number or bold claim as a hook must be in the approved facts.

**Example:** result-first hook for a SaaS: first frame "Invoice paid", then rewind to how.
