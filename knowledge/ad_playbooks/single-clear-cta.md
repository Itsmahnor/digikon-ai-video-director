title: One clear call to action
source: https://www.hooked.so/blog/script-for-an-advertisement
grade: C
topics: strategy,cta,end card
---
End with one action in plain words, shown long enough to read, ideally with the brand mark. Several competing actions weaken response. The call to action can be spoken, written or both, but must not contain a claim that has no approved brand fact.
