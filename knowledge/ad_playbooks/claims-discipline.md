---
id: claims-discipline
title: Only claim what is approved
source: Digikon policy
grade: A
topics: strategy,claims,legal
---
Every factual statement about the product, such as ingredients, results, prices, awards or health effects, must link to an approved brand fact. If no fact supports it, mark the claim unsupported and do not put it on screen or in the script. When style and legal text conflict, legal text and approved claims win; then product fidelity, platform specs, message clarity, pacing and style, in that order.
