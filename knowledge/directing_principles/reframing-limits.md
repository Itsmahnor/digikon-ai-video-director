title: Limits of reframing supplied footage
source: Digikon engineering note (benchmark to confirm)
grade: C
topics: cinematography,reframing,quality
---
We do not shoot new footage. A push-in, pan or crop is a crop of the source frame, so every zoom costs resolution. As a starting rule, keep total zoom within about 1.5x on 1080p source for a 1080p export, and flag the shot as not realisable if the source is lower resolution or the subject is already tight. Treat these limits as hypotheses until tested on real footage.
