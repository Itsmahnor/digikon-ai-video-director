title: On-screen text legibility
source: Digikon engineering note (benchmark to confirm)
grade: C
topics: motion,text,legibility
---
Keep lines short, hold each line long enough to read twice, and keep text inside the platform safe area. Platform safe-area values are not yet verified, so use the platform_specs collection when it has a verified entry, and otherwise keep text well away from the top and bottom edges. Contrast with the background matters more than font choice.
