Walter Murch ranks what a good cut should satisfy: emotion first, then story, rhythm, eye trace (where the viewer is looking), the flat screen plane, and 3D continuity of space. When two goals conflict, serve the higher one. For ads this means a cut that lands the intended feeling beats a cut that is technically smooth. His published weights are a heuristic, not a measured rule, so use the order and not the numbers.
