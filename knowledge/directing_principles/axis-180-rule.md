grade: B
topics: cinematography,continuity
---
Keep the camera on one side of an imaginary line between two subjects or along a subject's direction of travel, so left and right stay consistent between shots. When editing supplied footage, check that adjacent shots of the same action do not flip screen direction unless a neutral shot separates them.
