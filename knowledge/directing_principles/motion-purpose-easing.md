title: Purposeful motion and easing
source: https://design.gitlab.com/brand-design/motion
grade: B
topics: motion,animation,easing
---
Motion should explain something: what is new, what to look at, what changed. Ease in and out rather than moving linearly, keep durations short for small elements, and keep one dominant motion at a time so hierarchy stays clear. Repeating the same dramatic effect dilutes it.
