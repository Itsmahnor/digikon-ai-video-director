grade: A
topics: sound,loudness,ducking
---
Lower music under speech or key effects and restore it afterwards. EBU R 128 sets a broadcast target of -23 LUFS with a true-peak ceiling of -1 dBTP; it is a broadcast standard, not a social-platform target. Confirm the destination platform's own loudness guidance before fixing a target, and keep the true peak below the ceiling to avoid clipping on re-encode.
