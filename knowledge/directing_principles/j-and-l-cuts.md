title: J-cuts and L-cuts

topics: editing,transitions,audio
---
In a J-cut the next scene's audio starts before its picture; in an L-cut the previous scene's audio continues over the next picture. Both soften a cut and link two scenes through sound. Use a short lead of a few tenths of a second for product ads with music or speech; a longer overlap reads as a mistake. Needs source footage with usable audio.
